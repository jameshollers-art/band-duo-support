Privacy Policy

Effective October 17, 2026. This policy covers the Band Duo app for iPhone ("the app"), made by Jim Hollers.

The short version

Band Duo has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The app does not collect your name, email, contacts, location or photos.

Camera

If Play in your room is on, the app uses the camera to place the stage on a table and to let your hands appear in front of the notes. The camera images are processed only on your iPhone, in real time. They are never saved, recorded or sent anywhere. You can play without the camera by turning off Play in your room in Settings.

What stays on your iPhone

Your settings, timing calibration and best scores are stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, your scores and achievements are sent to Apple's Game Center so they can appear on leaderboards. Apple handles this data under Apple's Privacy Policy. We do not receive your Game Center contacts or account details.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Questions: send a support request.
